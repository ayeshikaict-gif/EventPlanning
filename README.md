# PlanVibe Lanka • Personal Event Planner Studio 🇱🇰🪷

**PlanVibe Lanka** is an interactive, attractive, and simple personal event planning application built for event planners in **Sri Lanka**. Any planner or user can personalize and store their own details, manage events, track budgets in Sri Lankan Rupees (LKR / Rs.), and export/backup their complete database file.

---

## 👤 Personal Profile & Data Ownership for Any User

1. **Planner Profile Settings (⚙️ Top Right Pill)**:
   - Click the profile avatar in the header to enter your own:
     - **Planner Full Name**
     - **Agency / Business Name**
     - **Phone / WhatsApp Number**
     - **Email Address**
     - **Operating District in Sri Lanka**
     - **Tagline / Specialization**
   - Stored securely in your browser's `localStorage` and dynamically displayed across the app and on printed briefs.

2. **Full Data Backup & Transfer (JSON)**:
   - **Download Backup**: Click **"Download Backup (JSON)"** to save a complete copy of all your events, checklists, guests, budgets, schedules, and profile to your computer.
   - **Restore from Backup**: Switch to another computer or browser, upload your JSON file, and all your events and profile restore instantly!

3. **Clean Blank Slate Option**:
   - Easily clear demo templates with one click (**"Start Blank Slate"**) to begin creating your own real events from scratch, while keeping the ability to restore sample templates whenever needed.

## 🇱🇰 Tailored for Sri Lanka

- **Currency in Sri Lankan Rupees (LKR / Rs.)**: Real-time budgeting and expense tracking in `Rs.` (`Rs. 4,800,000`, `Rs. 680,000`).
- **Authentic Sri Lankan Categories**:
  - 🪷 **Poruwa Wedding Ceremony** (Poruwa setup, auspicious Nekath times, Ashtaka, Jayamangala Gatha, Magul Bera, brass oil lamp, kiribath, wedding cake piece boxes).
  - 💍 **Homecoming Reception** (Second-day banquet, baila dance, grand buffet).
  - 🏆 **Corporate Gala & Awards** (BMICH, Cinnamon Grand, Shangri-La, Waters Edge).
  - 🎂 **Birthday & Milestone Party** (Puberty Kotahalu Mangallaya, 21st birthday, Golden Jubilee).
  - 🥥 **Beach Party & DJ Night** (Galle, Bentota, Mirissa, Negombo).
  - ☸️ **Pirith Chanting & Almsgiving (Dāna)** (Pirith Mandapaya, Sanghika Dāna, Pirikara offering).
  - 🏏 **Big Match / Cricket Fiesta** (Tents, papare brass band, celebration).
  - 🌞 **Avurudu Celebration** (Traditional games & sweetmeats).
- **Districts of Sri Lanka**: Built-in district filter and selector (Colombo, Kandy, Galle, Gampaha, Negombo, Matara, Kurunegala, etc.).
- **Sri Lankan Dietary RSVP Tracking**: Easy tagging for *Non-Veg*, *Pure Vegetarian*, *Halal*, and *Seafood Only*, plus table numbers and +1 counts.
- **Traditional Vendor Directory**: Directory for Magul Bera drummers, Poruwa craftsmen, Papare brass bands, saree drapers, and drone cinematography.
